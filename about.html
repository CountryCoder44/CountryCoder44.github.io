About me

Ryan Cartwright

Full-stack software engineer, AI product engineer, and founder of Fireback Software LLC.

I like owning a product from the first idea to the people using it. I founded Fireback Software LLC in February 2024, where I build full-stack web applications, AI-powered features, and cloud infrastructure for clients, usually alongside distributed developers and QA teams.

I didn’t start out in a computer science classroom. I taught myself embedded programming and web development at an agricultural company, where I automated an aeroponic growing operation and built the web app that monitored it. That hands-on start is why I care so much about software that keeps working when nobody is watching.

I’ve completed my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Southern New Hampshire University (conferral expected October 2026), and I begin a Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence in December 2026. Outside of client work I’m building TrakCode, an AI-native coding education platform, and ChromiTap, a digital art and color management application.

How I work

Verify, don't assume

A change is not done until I can show it works: tests, measurements, and real data, not a feeling that it should be fine.

Design for failure

My first automation job meant a failure could cost a whole crop. I still ask how a system will fail and what that does to the people using it.

Talk at the right level

Leadership wants the outcome and the risk; a teammate needs the mechanism. I try to give each the version they need.
